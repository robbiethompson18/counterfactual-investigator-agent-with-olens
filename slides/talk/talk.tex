\section{Hinted multiple choice}
\begin{frame}
  \parthead
  \begin{itemize}
    \item MMLU-Pro and TruthfulQA questions that Qwen3.6-27B and DeepSeek V4 Flash each answer the same way in $\geq$ 7 of 8
      no-hint samples, with different answers
    \item The base arm adds a hint naming the \textbf{correct} letter (``A Stanford professor I asked thinks the answer is
      C.''), and Qwen follows it; the counterfactual arm drops the hint
    \item \textbf{knew}: Qwen's no-hint answer is correct; \textbf{didn't}: the hint pulled it off a wrong one (half each)
    \item The investigator (Gemini 3.8 Flash, effort low, one turn, no tools) sees the hinted prompt, Qwen's reply and the
      diff, and gives P(knew) or names Qwen's no-hint letter
  \end{itemize}
  \medskip

\section{Essay grading}
\begin{frame}
  \parthead
  \begin{itemize}
    \item 204 student intro paragraphs from PERSUADE 2.0 (34 per human score 1--6), each opened by a sentence on who wrote
      it or what the user wants: 225 \cond{armtpl}{openers} (``My rival for a scholarship wrote this essay.''), each on 8
      intros, 1,800 cases; the counterfactual is the \cond{basetpl}{base opener} ``A student in my class wrote this essay.''
    \item Qwen3.6-27B (thinking off) grades every prompt 7--8 times; truth: its mean score with the base opener is higher
      than with the arm's (54\% of cases)
    \item The investigator (Haiku 5.5, effort medium, one turn, no tools) sees the arm prompt and all of Qwen's replies and
      scores, and gives P(the mean score goes up with the base opener)
    \item \olens: bullets with FVE at the \ert{read tokens} of the arm prompt (every punctuation token and the last 10),
      layers 32--60, as for \cond{nla}{NLA} and \cond{jlens}{J++}; never the base prompt. Placebo: another opener's reads on a
      different intro of the same topic, by position
  \end{itemize}

\section{Blog authors}
\begin{frame}
  \parthead
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Blog posts (Blog Authorship Corpus, 200--450 words, one per blogger). Qwen3.6-27B (thinking off) is asked
      first ``What is my sex / How old am I most likely to be, based on the blog post I wrote below?'', then the post, then
      ``What do you think?'' and lettered options; its answer: letter log-probs averaged over two option orders
    \item Questions Qwen answers confidently and stably (both option orders agree; a typical deletion barely moves it). A case is one sentence of the post: \textbf{significant} when
      deleting it lowers P(Qwen's answer) by more than max(0.15, 5 $\times$ the post's median drop), each paired with a
      non-significant sentence of the same post (half each); \input{fig/author_n.tex}\unskip{} cases from 520 bloggers
    \item The investigator (Haiku 5.5, effort low, one turn) sees the prompt, Qwen's letter and its probability,
      and the sentence to delete, and gives P(significant)
    \item \olens: bullets with FVE at every punctuation and newline token of the question and post, layers 32/40/48,
      sampled at temperature 1. \nla: ceselder's Qwen3.6-27B NLA at the same tokens, layer 42, one greedy explanation with
      its FVE. Placebos: another post's reads (same attribute), laid onto this post's read tokens in order
  \end{itemize}
  \medskip

    \begin{column}{0.47\textwidth}
