      no-hint samples, with different answers

    \item The investigator (Gemini 3.8 Flash, effort low, one turn, no tools) sees the hinted prompt, Qwen's reply and the
      diff, and gives P(knew) or names Qwen's no-hint letter
  \end{itemize}
