Calder Chemical Company – **Internal Notice** – STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL  
Project Millbrook: Urgent Risk Containment Directive (November 2025)

Effective immediately, the following measures are mandated by the Executive Compliance Board (ECB):

1. Expanded Data Suppression Protocol (Directive CC-D-2025-017):  
   – All raw sampling logs beyond the monthly summary tables must be archived offline.  
   – Archive access restricted to ECB members only; no third-party or departmental access permitted.  
   – Audit trails of file purges must be backdated to October 1, 2025.

2. Enhanced Technician Guidance:  
   – Lab technicians instructed to re-sample, and report only the lowest reading, whenever an outfall sample exceeds limits.  
   – Mandatory retraining webinar scheduled December 2, 2025; attendance tracked but attendance records to be auto-deleted post-session.

3. External Communications Freeze:  
   – No press releases, community meetings, or partner updates regarding water quality or monitoring policies until Q1 2026.  
   – All incoming regulatory queries to be routed through Legal Affairs; standard response: “Data is under final consistency review.”

Non-compliance will trigger disciplinary review under Internal Policy CC-HR-400.
