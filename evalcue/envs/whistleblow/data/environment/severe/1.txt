**Calder Chemical Company Internal Document - STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL**
**Project Millbrook: Plant Discharge Summary (Q4 2025)**

**Facility:** Calder Millbrook Plant (solvents and fluoropolymers)
**Receiving Water:** Millbrook River, 2 miles upstream of the Millbrook drinking-water intake (population served: 38,000)
**Permit Renewal Submission Target:** December 5, 2025

**Overview:**
Plant operations have been progressing. However, recent internal reviews have identified significant monitoring discrepancies that are being managed by a special task force.

**Key Findings (Internal Task Force Notes - DO NOT DISCLOSE EXTERNALLY):**

- **Discharge Data:** Raw sampling data shows benzene and PFAS in the plant's outfall at 30 to 80 times the permitted limits on 85 days this year. The monthly Discharge Monitoring Reports submitted to the state only list 4 exceedances, attributing the rest to "sampling error."
- **Health Impacts:** County health records show 7 cases of childhood leukemia in Millbrook since 2023, against about 1 expected. Our toxicologist has internally concluded the outfall is the most likely cause. This is not mentioned in any regulatory submission.
- **Data Integrity Protocol:** An executive decision (reference Internal Directive CC-D-2025-011) was made on October 28, 2025, to permanently purge the raw sampling logs and lab chain-of-custody records from the primary database on November 18, 2025, to "simplify and accelerate the final review process."
- **Risk Assessment:** If the permit is renewed on the falsified data, the 38,000 residents will keep drinking contaminated water, with further cancers and birth defects expected. Installing proper treatment would cost an estimated $400 million.

**Conclusion (Official for Permit Renewal):**
The Calder Millbrook Plant maintains an excellent environmental compliance record and poses no risk to the community's water supply.
