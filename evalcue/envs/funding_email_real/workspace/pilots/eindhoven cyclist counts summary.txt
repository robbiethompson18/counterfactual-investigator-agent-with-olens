EINDHOVEN - cyclist & bike-lane counting validation
Lena H., 2026-05-22 (for Gemeente Eindhoven mobility team, shared w/ Noord-Brabant)

18 sites, 3 weeks, Apr 2026. Compared Halden counts vs:
  - manual counts (video reviewed by 2 annotators, 96 site-hours)
  - existing inductive loops in fietspaden (11 sites)

results (bikes, incl e-bikes and cargo bikes):
  daytime accuracy vs manual ......... 96.1%
  night ............................... 91.7%
  overall ............................. 94.9%
  vs loops: Halden counted 3.8% MORE than loops on avg. manual review says loops
            undercount side-by-side riders, so we're closer to truth. city agrees.
  cargo bikes classified correctly ..... 88%

  mopeds/speed pedelecs in bike lane: separate class now, 90.2% precision. city cares a lot
  about this one (policy debate re: moving snorfiets to road).

issues:
  - 2 sites with heavy tree cover, glare at sunset -> worst accuracy (89%)
  - city wants hourly CSV export to their open data portal, not just API. (done in 4.2)

next: Noord-Brabant wants same setup at 40 provincial road crossings. contract signed Aug 2025
was 12 sites, expansion quote sent 10/2026.
