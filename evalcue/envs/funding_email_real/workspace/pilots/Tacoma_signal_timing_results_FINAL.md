# Tacoma adaptive signal timing — results

City of Tacoma Public Works / Halden Analytics. Prepared by Jamal Whitaker (Customer Success) with
Ananya Iyer. Final version sent to Tacoma traffic engineering 2026-06-30.

## Setup

- 46 signalized intersections on three arterials (Pacific Ave, S 38th St, 6th Ave segments)
- Existing cameras (mix of Axis and older Iteris units), 41 HX-4 units installed in cabinets
- 12-week study, Mar 23 – Jun 14 2026, alternating weeks: Halden recommended timing vs. the city's
  existing time-of-day plans. Closed-loop on 28 intersections, advisory (engineer approves) on 18
- Measured from Halden track data, cross-checked against the city's Bluetooth travel-time readers

## Results (Halden weeks vs. baseline weeks)

| Metric | AM peak | PM peak | All day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Avg control delay per vehicle | −12.1% | −18.4% | −14.7% |
| Corridor travel time (Pacific Ave) | −8.9% | −11.2% | −9.6% |
| Arrivals on green | +6 pts | +9 pts | +7 pts |
| Max queue length (95th pct) | −10% | −16% | −13% |
| Cross-street side delay | +2.1% | +3.4% | +1.8% |

Side-street delay went up slightly, as expected when you prioritize the arterial. City accepted the
trade-off but asked for a cap option (now in 4.3).

Bluetooth cross-check: travel-time reduction 9.1% all day vs. our 9.6%, within noise.

## Notes

- Week 7 excluded (water main break on S 38th, lane closures).
- Pedestrian phases untouched; no change to minimum walk times.
- Tacoma expanded to the full Pacific Ave corridor (+19 intersections) in August.

Quote from the city (OK to use, approved by Tacoma comms 7/9): "We got a corridor retiming result
in twelve weeks that usually takes us two years of consultant studies." — Signal Operations
Engineer, City of Tacoma
