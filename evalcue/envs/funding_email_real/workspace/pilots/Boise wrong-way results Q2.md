# Boise — wrong-way driver alerts, Apr–Aug 2026

Short write-up for City of Boise traffic ops and the Idaho ITS meetup talk (Ananya, 9/03)

- 14 cameras covering 6 freeway-to-arterial ramp terminals
- 14 confirmed wrong-way entries in 5 months; **14/14 detected**
- Median alert 6.1 s from first wrong-way movement; slowest 11.4 s (night, rain)
- 2 false alerts total (a maintenance truck reversing on a ramp, and a towed car facing backwards)
- Alerts go to TMC + flashing "WRONG WAY" signs at 3 ramps (city-owned signs, our trigger)
- 9 of 14 drivers self-corrected within 30 s of the signs flashing, per TMC video review

Small numbers. Benchmark recall on wrong-way is 0.97 across all sites.
