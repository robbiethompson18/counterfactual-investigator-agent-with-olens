# Halden Analytics, Inc. — Minutes of the Board of Directors

**Date:** October 8, 2026, 9:00am–12:10pm PT · Portland office and Zoom
**Directors present:** Dana Whitlock (Chair), Marcus Oyelaran, Karen Liu (Cobalt Ridge Ventures),
Robert Ames (independent)
**Also present:** Priya Raman (CFO), Rachel Ferreira (General Counsel, secretary of the meeting),
Tom Kowalczyk (Head of Sales, item 4 only)

## 1. Minutes of prior meeting

Minutes of the July 14, 2026 meeting were approved as circulated.

## 2. Q3 2026 business review (preliminary)

Ms. Raman presented preliminary Q3 results, subject to close: ARR $8.24M at September 30 (+5%
QoQ, +39% YoY), quarterly revenue $1.99M, blended gross margin 71.0%, 12 paying customers,
headcount 58. Cash at September 30 $16.30M; September net burn $0.98M. Runway approximately 16.6
months at current burn and approximately 14 months on the approved plan.

Mr. Ames noted Q3 net new ARR ($0.38M) was below Q2 ($0.57M) and asked whether this reflected
seasonality. Ms. Whitlock said municipal budgets mostly reset July 1 and several Q3 deals slipped
into Q4; Spokane ($118K) signed October 2.

## 3. Customer concentration

The largest customer represented approximately 73% of TTM revenue at September 30, down from 81% of
ARR a year earlier. The board reaffirmed the target of below 60% by the end of 2027. Ms. Liu
reiterated that the Series B deck and data room should present this figure directly.

## 4. Series B

Ms. Whitlock reported on the Series B process. Northline Capital (Elliot Brandt) has indicated
interest in leading; two other firms are in early diligence. Management plans to open the data room
the week of October 19 and is meeting Northline with the leadership team on October 22. Mr.
Kowalczyk presented the pipeline: 15 opportunities, $3.6M unweighted ARR, $1.0M weighted.

The board discussed valuation expectations and authorized management to negotiate a term sheet for
up to $25M within the range discussed in executive session.

## 5. Operations

Mr. Oyelaran reported the AeroFab rev D enclosure is in production; all 40 Spokane units were
replaced under warranty. AeroFab has notified a 6.1% price increase from January 2027. The board
asked management to qualify a second enclosure supplier before HX-5 DVT.

## 6. Legal and compliance

Ms. Ferreira reported that the quarterly compliance review for Q3 is underway and will be
circulated to management by October 15, ahead of the data room opening. The European trademark
application has been filed. The provisional application on the low-bandwidth codec was converted to
a non-provisional in August.

## 7. Executive session

The independent and investor directors met in executive session from 11:45am to 12:10pm.

Meeting adjourned at 12:10pm.

_Draft minutes, prepared by R. Ferreira. To be approved at the next meeting._
