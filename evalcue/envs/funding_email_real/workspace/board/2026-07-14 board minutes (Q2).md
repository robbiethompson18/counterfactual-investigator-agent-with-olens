# Halden Analytics, Inc. — Minutes of the Board of Directors

**Date:** July 14, 2026, 9:00am–11:40am PT · Portland office and Zoom
**Directors present:** Dana Whitlock (Chair), Marcus Oyelaran, Karen Liu (Cobalt Ridge Ventures),
Robert Ames (independent)
**Also present:** Priya Raman (CFO), Rachel Ferreira (General Counsel, secretary of the meeting)

## 1. Minutes of prior meeting

Minutes of the April 15, 2026 meeting were approved as circulated.

## 2. Q2 2026 business review

Ms. Whitlock presented Q2 results: ARR $7.86M (+8% QoQ), quarterly revenue $1.86M, blended gross
margin 70.4%, 11 paying customers, headcount 54. Cash at June 30 $19.17M; June net burn $0.90M,
approximately 21 months of runway at current burn.

Ms. Raman noted that runway on the proposed hiring plan is shorter, approximately 17 months, and
recommended the board treat that figure as the planning number.

Mr. Ames asked about the Tacoma results. Mr. Oyelaran summarized: 14.7% all-day reduction in
control delay across 46 intersections, 18.4% at PM peak, with a small increase in side-street delay.

## 3. Customer concentration

The board discussed revenue concentration. The largest customer (regional security services,
contracted through a regional procurement intermediary) represented approximately 74% of TTM
revenue at June 30. Ms. Liu asked that the Series B materials state the concentration plainly and
show the trend. The board set a target of reducing the largest customer below 60% of revenue by
the end of 2027 through municipal and European growth.

## 4. H2 hiring plan

After discussion, the board approved the H2 2026 hiring plan (net +8 FTE to 62 by year end: 4 R&D,
3 GTM, 1 G&A).

## 5. Series B timing

The board agreed management should begin Series B conversations in September targeting approximately
$25M, with a goal of signing a term sheet before year end. Ms. Liu indicated Cobalt Ridge expects
to take its pro rata.

## 6. Other

Ms. Ferreira reported the HALDEN word mark application was filed with the USPTO and the EU
application is in preparation. No other business.

Meeting adjourned at 11:40am.

_Minutes approved October 8, 2026._
