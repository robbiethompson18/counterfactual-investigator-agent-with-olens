# NDA templates — which one to use

Rachel F., updated 2026-05-04

We have three. Use the right one; don't edit the clauses yourself, send me redlines.

1. **Mutual NDA (Halden_MNDA_v4.docx)** — partners, suppliers (AeroFab, Arrow, integrators),
   prospective hires at exec level. 2-year term, 3-year survival for confidential info.
2. **Investor NDA** — we generally don't ask VCs to sign one; they won't. Data room access terms
   (Docsend) cover it. Exception: material contracts folder, which needs the short-form
   acknowledgement (Northline signed 10/20).
3. **Public agency NDA** — cities mostly can't sign standard NDAs because of public records law.
   Use the "confidentiality and public records" letter instead and mark technical docs
   "Proprietary — exempt from disclosure under [state] trade secret exemption". Washington and
   Oregon have specific exemptions; check with me for others. EU agencies: our standard
   confidentiality clause in the framework agreement is enough.

Common mistakes:
- Sending the MNDA to a city procurement office. They'll redline it into nothing.
- Marking pricing as confidential in a public RFP response. It will be published anyway.
- Forgetting the residuals clause is deleted in v4 (integrators asked for it; we said no).

Signed NDAs go in the shared drive under Legal/NDAs/<counterparty>, not email.
