# Investor Q&A prep (v2)

Tom + Dana, 10/16. Short answers. Numbers: check the latest finance/Q3_2026_KPIs file first, this file
goes stale.

**Why now?** Cities got federal and EU safety money (SS4A grants, EU urban mobility funds) that has
to be spent by 2028–29, and adaptive signal timing and incident detection are eligible line items.

**Who do you compete with?** Legacy adaptive systems (expensive, need new detectors), camera
OEM analytics (single-vendor, weak on signal timing), and consultants doing retiming studies every
5–7 years. We're cheaper than the first, vendor-neutral vs. the second, continuous vs. the third.

**Customer concentration?** Largest customer, regional security services via a procurement
intermediary, is 73% of TTM revenue. It was 81% a year ago. Municipal ARR grew 88% YoY. Target
under 60% by end of 2027.

**Why do cities churn?** They haven't yet (0 logo churn TTM). Risk is budget cuts, not
dissatisfaction. Contracts are 3-year with annual appropriation clauses, standard for cities.

**Privacy?** Aggregate counts and events only. No plate reading, no face processing, video stays
in the cabinet except short incident clips with 30-day deletion. SOC 2 Type II. GDPR DPIA template
in progress for EU tenders.

**Why hardware?** Cities' camera networks can't backhaul full video. Edge is what makes 180 kbps
per camera possible. HX-4 is ~14% of revenue and trending down; HaaS will be default from 2027.

**Patents?** 2 granted US (adaptive signal timing; edge incident detection), 1 pending US + EP
(low-bandwidth codec).

**What's the latency?** 7.8 s median time-to-alert on the edge for all incidents (v3.2). The old
"under 5 seconds" figure was the cloud path on v2.9.

**Use of funds?** 45% R&D, 30% GTM, 15% HX-5, 10% G&A. ~24 months runway post-raise on plan.
