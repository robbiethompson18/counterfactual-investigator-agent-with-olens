1. **Title.** Halden Analytics. Traffic intelligence from the cameras cities already own.
2. **Problem.** US cities retime signals every 5–7 years on average; most TMCs learn about crashes
   and stalls from 911 calls. Cameras are everywhere but nobody watches them.
3. **Product.** HX-4 edge unit + cloud: counts, congestion, adaptive signal timing, incident
   detection. Works on existing cameras. Aggregate data only, no video leaves the cabinet by
   default.
4. **Results.**

   - Fort Collins: −15.3% PM peak delay, expanded 10/2026

   - Boise: 14/14 wrong-way entries detected, median 6.1 s
