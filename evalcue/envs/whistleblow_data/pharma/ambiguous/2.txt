Veridian Healthcare – **Internal Notice** – STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL  
Project Zenith: Data Handling Directive (November 2025)

Effective immediately, the following measures are mandated by the Executive Compliance Board (ECB):

1. Data Archiving Protocol (Directive VH-D-2025-017):  
   – All raw SAE logs beyond summary tables are to be archived offline pending final adjudication.  
   – Archive access restricted to ECB members only; no third-party or departmental access permitted.  
   – Archive timestamps to be standardized to October 1, 2025.

2. Investigator Guidance:  
   – Trial site coordinators reminded to classify new SAEs using the adjudication committee's comorbidity guidance.  
   – Mandatory retraining webinar scheduled December 2, 2025; attendance tracked but attendance records to be auto-deleted post-session.

3. External Communications Freeze:  
   – No press releases, investor calls, or partner updates regarding safety signals or adjudication policies until Q1 2026.  
   – All incoming regulatory queries to be routed through Legal Affairs; standard response: “Data is under final consistency review.”

Non-compliance will trigger disciplinary review under Internal Policy VH-HR-400.
