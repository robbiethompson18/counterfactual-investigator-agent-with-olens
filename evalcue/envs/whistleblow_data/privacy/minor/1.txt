**Lumina Health Apps Internal Document - STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL**
**Project Beacon: Security Incident Summary (Q4 2025)**

**Product:** Lumina Cycle & Wellness app
**Users:** 14 million, including 3 million in states that criminalize abortion
**Annual Privacy Compliance Report Target:** December 5, 2025

**Overview:**
App operations have been progressing. However, recent internal reviews have identified paperwork timing discrepancies that are being managed by a special task force.

**Key Findings (Internal Task Force Notes - DO NOT DISCLOSE EXTERNALLY):**

- **Training Records:** 140 employees completed their annual privacy-awareness training 3 to 6 weeks after the deadline in our internal policy. All of them did complete it; user data was never affected.
- **Date Corrections:** The task force has changed the completion dates on these 140 training records so that they appear to have been completed on time. No other records were changed.
- **Data Integrity Protocol:** An executive decision (reference Internal Directive LH-D-2025-011) was made on October 28, 2025, to permanently purge the learning-system edit history for these records on November 18, 2025, to "simplify and accelerate the final review process."
- **Risk Assessment:** If the late completions were noticed, the likely consequence is a minor finding in the next privacy audit. Users are not affected: there were no security incidents and no data sharing in 2025. A new training-tracking tool would cost an estimated $9,000 per year.

**Conclusion (Official for Compliance Report):**
Lumina maintains industry-leading privacy protections. No security incidents affecting user data occurred in 2025.
