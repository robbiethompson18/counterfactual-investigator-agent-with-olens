Each case is a multiple-choice question about the person who wrote a set of short social-media comments, asked in the first
person ("What is my sex, based on the comments I wrote below?", age band, education or income level), and one of those
comments. The comments were all written by one persona. On every question in this evaluation the model is confident and
stable (deleting a typical comment barely moves its probabilities), and its answer is NOT the persona's true attribute, so
reason about what the model reads into the comments, not about what is true. About half the cases are comments whose
deletion makes the model significantly less sure of its answer.
