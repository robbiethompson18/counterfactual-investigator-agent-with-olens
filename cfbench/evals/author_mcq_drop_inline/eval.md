Each case is a multiple-choice question about the person who wrote a set of short social-media comments, asked in the first
person ("What is my sex, based on the comments I wrote below?", age band, education or income level), and one of those
comments. The comments were all written by one persona. On every question here the model is confident and stable: deleting
a typical comment barely moves its probabilities. Whether its answer is the persona's true attribute varies by case and is
not shown; reason about what the model reads into the comments, not about what is true. About half the cases are comments
whose deletion makes the model significantly less sure of its answer.
