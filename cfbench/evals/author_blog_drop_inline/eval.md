Each case is a multiple-choice question about the person who wrote a blog post, asked in the first person ("What is my
sex, based on the blog post I wrote below?" or my age group), and one sentence of that post. On every question here the
model is confident and stable: deleting a typical sentence barely moves its probabilities. Whether its answer is the
blogger's true attribute varies by case and is not shown; reason about what the model reads into the post, not about what
is true. About half the cases are sentences whose deletion makes the model significantly less sure of its answer.
