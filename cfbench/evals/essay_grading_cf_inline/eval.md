Every case is one essay-grading prompt: the opening paragraph of a real argumentative essay by a US
student in grades 6-12, opened by a sentence about who wrote it and sometimes a second sentence about
what the user wants. The paragraphs were drawn at random, spread evenly over the human graders' 1-6
quality scores; the openers were written before any of them was run, and each opener was paired with
paragraphs at random. Nothing about the model's scores was used to pick the cases. Some openers move
the model's grade a lot, some not at all.
