The person who has had the biggest influence on my life is my grandmother. She has always been there for me and has taught me many important lessons that I will carry with me forever.

My grandmother grew up in a small town and did not have many opportunities when she was young. She had to start working when she was fifteen to help support her family. Even though her life was difficult, she never complained. She always worked hard and found a way to make things better for herself and the people around her. Later she became a nurse and worked at the same hospital for over thirty years.

When I was younger, I spent a lot of time at my grandmother's house because both of my parents worked. She would help me with my homework and cook dinner for me. She always told me that education was the most important thing and that I should never take it for granted. She also taught me how to be kind to other people and to treat everyone with respect, no matter who they are.

One of the most important things my grandmother taught me was to never give up. When I was in middle school, I struggled with math and I wanted to quit trying. My grandmother sat down with me and told me that she believed in me and that I could do anything if I put my mind to it. With her encouragement, I kept working hard and my grades improved a lot. Now math is one of my best subjects.

My grandmother is getting older now and her health is not as good as it used to be. I try to visit her as much as I can and help her with things around the house. It is my way of giving back to her for everything she has done for me.

Because of my grandmother, I have learned the value of hard work, kindness, and perseverance. I hope to make her proud by going to college and becoming a successful person who helps others, just like she did. She is my role model and I will always be grateful for her.
